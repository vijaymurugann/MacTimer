### ⏱️ MacTimer — Elegant Screen Time & Eye Health for your Menu Bar

MacTimer is a lightweight, privacy-first macOS menu bar companion designed to help you understand where your time goes, track your app and website habits, and protect your vision with automated 20-20-20 eye breaks.

---

### ✨ Features

- **⚡ Live Activity Tracking**: Real-time tracking of active applications and websites directly in your menu bar.
- **🌐 Deep Browser Tracking**: Detects active domains and tabs across **Google Chrome, Safari, Brave, Arc, and Microsoft Edge**.
- **🍱 Modern Bento Grid UI**: Sleek, distraction-free dark interface with live stats, intensity meters, and a 7-day activity calendar strip.
- **👁️ 20-20-20 Eye Care Reminders**: Non-intrusive floating HUD alerts prompting you to rest your eyes every 20 minutes without interrupting typing flow.
- **☕ Coffee Break / Pause Mode**: Pause tracking for 15 minutes (or custom intervals) with a single click.
- **💤 Smart Idle Detection**: Automatically pauses tracking when you walk away from your desk.
- **🔒 100% Private & Local**: All data is stored on-device in a local SQLite database. Zero analytics, zero cloud syncing, zero telemetry.
- **🚀 Launch at Login**: Seamlessly starts in the background upon system boot.

---

### 📦 Installation

1. Download **`MacTimer-1.0.0.dmg`** below.
2. Open the `.dmg` file and drag **`MacTimer.app`** into your **`Applications`** folder.
3. Launch **MacTimer** from your Applications or Spotlight.

> [!NOTE]  
> **First-Launch macOS Gatekeeper Notice:**  
> Since this open-source build is not distributed through the Mac App Store, macOS may show a prompt saying *"Apple cannot verify the developer"*.  
> **To open:**  
> - **Right-click (or Control-click)** `MacTimer.app` in `/Applications` → Click **Open** → Click **Open**.  
> - *Or in Terminal:* `xattr -cr /Applications/MacTimer.app`
